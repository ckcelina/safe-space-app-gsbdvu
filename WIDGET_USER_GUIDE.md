
# Safe Space Widget - User Guide

## What is the Safe Space Widget?

The Safe Space widget is a Home Screen widget for iPhone that displays your Safe Space logo with your chosen theme. It provides quick access to the app and automatically updates its appearance when you change your theme.

## Adding the Widget to Your Home Screen

### Step 1: Enter Edit Mode
1. Long press on an empty area of your Home Screen
2. Your apps will start wiggling

### Step 2: Open Widget Gallery
1. Tap the "+" button in the top-left corner
2. The widget gallery will open

### Step 3: Find Safe Space Widget
1. Scroll down or search for "Safe Space"
2. Tap on the Safe Space widget

### Step 4: Choose Widget Size
- **Small**: Shows just the Safe Space logo
- **Medium**: Shows logo with "Safe Space" and "Check in" text

### Step 5: Add to Home Screen
1. Swipe left/right to preview different sizes
2. Tap "Add Widget"
3. Position the widget where you want it
4. Tap "Done" in the top-right corner

## Customizing Your Widget

### Changing the Theme

The widget automatically updates to match your app theme:

1. Open the Safe Space app
2. Go to **Settings** (bottom navigation)
3. Tap **Theme Selection**
4. Choose your theme:
   - **Ocean Blue** - Calm and serene
   - **Soft Rose** - Gentle and nurturing (default)
   - **Forest Green** - Grounded and peaceful
   - **Sunny Yellow** - Bright and uplifting
5. Return to your Home Screen
6. The widget will update within a few seconds

### Widget Sizes

**Small Widget (2x2)**
- Perfect for minimal Home Screen layouts
- Shows Safe Space logo only
- Gradient background matches your theme

**Medium Widget (4x2)**
- More prominent display
- Shows logo, app name, and "Check in" text
- Great for quick access

## Using the Widget

### Opening the App
- Tap anywhere on the widget to open Safe Space
- You'll be taken directly to the app

### Widget Updates
- The widget updates automatically when you change themes
- No manual refresh needed
- Updates happen within seconds

## Troubleshooting

### Widget Not Updating

**If your widget doesn't update after changing themes:**

1. **Wait a moment** - Updates can take 5-10 seconds
2. **Force refresh** - Remove and re-add the widget
3. **Restart app** - Force quit Safe Space and reopen
4. **Restart iPhone** - Sometimes iOS needs a restart

### Widget Shows Wrong Theme

**If the widget shows a different theme than your app:**

1. Open Safe Space app
2. Go to Settings → Theme Selection
3. Select your desired theme again
4. Wait 10 seconds
5. Check Home Screen

### Widget Not Appearing in Gallery

**If you can't find the Safe Space widget:**

1. Make sure you have the latest version of Safe Space
2. Restart your iPhone
3. Check that you're running iOS 14 or later
4. Reinstall the app if necessary

### Widget Shows Default Theme

**If the widget always shows pink (Soft Rose):**

1. This is the default theme
2. Open the app and select a different theme
3. The widget will update to match

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I customize the widget text?
Not currently. The widget shows "Safe Space" and "Check in" on medium size, and just the logo on small size.

### Does the widget work on iPad?
Yes! The widget works on iPad with the same features.

### Does the widget drain battery?
No. The widget is very efficient and has minimal battery impact.

### Can I have different themes for app and widget?
No. The widget always matches your app theme for consistency.

### How often does the widget update?
The widget updates immediately when you change themes, and refreshes automatically every hour.

### Does the widget work offline?
Yes! The widget works completely offline and doesn't require internet.

### Can I interact with the widget?
Currently, tapping the widget opens the app. More interactive features may come in future updates.

### Why does my widget look different from screenshots?
The widget appearance changes based on your selected theme. This is intentional and allows personalization.

## Getting Help

3. Try removing and re-adding the widget

5. Contact support through the app (Settings → Support)
